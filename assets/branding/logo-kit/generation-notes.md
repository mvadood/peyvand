# Transparent cutout trials

Mode: built-in image generation.

These trials were inspected and discarded because of edge artifacts. They are not the production exports. The selected original concept was converted to native SVG paths, and all final variations are direct exports from that master.

## transparent-dark

Use case: background-extraction / production logo variant.
Edit the supplied approved Peyvand logo. Produce a faithful production PNG with a REAL TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (alpha channel), including the spaces between all strokes and letter counters. Do not draw a background or a checkerboard pattern.
Preserve the exact geometry, outlines, relative positions, angular terminals, smooth curves, separate two-stroke symbol, smaller Persian wordmark, dot positions and counts, symbol-to-text scale, and tight portrait proportions. The only text is the original "پیوند". No Iran detail. No human profiles. Do not re-typeset or redesign anything. Every edge must be clean and antialiased, with NO grey fringe, white halo, shadows, noise, speckles, glow, texture or gradients. Flat opaque artwork on transparent background. Keep approximately the existing small safe margin around the complete logo.
Set ALL artwork to a uniform solid near-black #15181B. This version is intended for light backgrounds. Output only the single full logo.
