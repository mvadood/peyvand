## Exact generation prompt

Use case: precise logo edit.
Image 1 is the target logo. Image 2 is a geographic reference only: Iran's outline on a transparent background.

Preserve Image 1 EXACTLY: the two original abstract near-black strokes, their smooth inner and outer contours, every proportion and position, tight portrait crop, cool pale-grey background, and the smaller Persian wordmark "پیوند" with all its letterforms and dots.

Add ONE very small hidden detail: a tiny Iran-shaped negative-space aperture, cut out of the broad solid area in the UPPER/LEFT abstract stroke. The aperture must be the same pale cool grey as the background, a true visual hole in the dark stroke, without any outline or added colour. Use a simplified silhouette of Iran's geographic boundary guided by Image 2, retaining recognizable broad geographic proportions and north-up orientation, without detailed border wiggles. Do not use a generic diamond, star, face, bird or random blob. Its width should be only about 12% of the total emblem width, and it must fit fully inside the broad stroke with ample black surrounding it. It should feel like a small typographic counter that you discover only when looking closely.

This is the only change. Do not try to make the large central gap into a map. Do not warp or notch the existing emblem's edges; they must remain smooth and abstract exactly as in Image 1. Do not add any facial features, flag, third logo component, label or explanatory text. Do not enlarge the geographic detail: it is a tiny embedded signature, not the main symbol. The dominant visual remains the existing two-stroke conversation mark.

Flat near-black #15181B on fully opaque cool pale-grey #E9ECEF, sharp clean edges, no shading, texture, gradients, shadows or transparency. Keep the Persian text untouched.
