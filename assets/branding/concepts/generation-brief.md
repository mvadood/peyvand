## Prompts

### 01-modern-wordmark

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: first-round logo concept for a Persian conversational podcast about books and ideas called پیوند.
Text verbatim: "پیوند". It must be correct, readable Persian, right to left. Letters in logical order: پ ی و ن د. Preserve natural Persian joining, the non-joining boundary after و, and every required letter dot. The ONLY text in the image is پیوند, exactly once. No Latin lettering, subtitles, taglines, option numbers, or extra words.
Presentation: one centered logo composition on a completely flat deep charcoal-navy background #111923, ample clean negative space, square image. Crisp flat vector-like artwork, no texture, gradients, shadows, 3D, photography, mockups, ornamented borders, or watermarks.
Mood: thoughtful, welcoming, contemporary editorial identity with quiet confidence; connection between people and ideas. It must work across many episodes, with a strong silhouette and practical small-size readability. Avoid microphones, headphones, generic podcast waves, literal chain links, and imagery from any specific book.
Direction: A distinctive pure Persian typographic wordmark. Bold, beautifully balanced contemporary Persian lettering with gently rounded terminals and open counters. Clean restrained customized letterforms, optically spaced with natural script connections; no separate icon. Main letters warm ivory #F5EBD7; a restrained amber #F3BF5E accent may highlight the existing three dots of پ without changing their positions or number. Strong horizontal wordmark, dominant and legible. The idea of connection comes from harmonious connected strokes, not added illustration.
