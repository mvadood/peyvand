## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: one concept example of an empty photographic background plate for Peyvand, a serious contemporary Persian conversational podcast about books. Landscape 16:9 composition.
Create a believable contemporary reading room, photographed from a seated person's eye level with a normal 40mm lens, facing the background wall several metres behind where speakers will later be composited. An intimate lived-in cultural space with sophisticated natural materials: gently textured cool grey mineral plaster, deep walnut built-in shelves concentrated toward the outer sides, a modest irregular selection of actual-looking used books with muted spines, one dark handmade ceramic vessel, a small understated Persian ceramic object. Soft lateral daylight from a tall window just outside the left edge gives the wall attractive shadows and depth; slight reflected warmth from walnut, no orange colour wash. On the far right a partial view into a deeper alcove, a muted olive plant and a small shaded practical lamp, all restrained.
The frame should feel interesting, grounded and contemporary, quietly Persian through craftsmanship rather than symbols. Editorial architectural photography, authentic material imperfections, elegant but not a luxury showroom. Calm mid-frame behind future heads with tonal texture and light, not a large empty featureless wall. Detail and shelving toward the edges; medium depth of field gently softening background objects while materials stay legible. Moody neutral charcoal, stone grey and natural walnut, luminous enough for a human conversation, energetic light/shadow contrast. No people, faces, mannequins, foreground furniture, microphones, chairs or table. No graphic overlays, words, readable book titles, logos, national symbols, map, flags, arches, neon, decorative LED strips, video-call panels, borders or collage. One cohesive room and one camera angle filling the image. This is a quick visual concept of the background alone, not a full podcast layout.
